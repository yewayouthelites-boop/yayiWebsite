The Legislative Record

Six consecutive election victories across two states, and a career spent in the rooms where Nigeria's money is watched, raised and shared.

2003–2011 · Lagos State House of Assembly

Member, Alimosho Constituency II

Two terms in the Lagos Assembly, where he was instrumental in the tax administration reforms that grew Lagos IRS monthly collections from ₦5 billion to ₦20 billion, the fiscal foundation of modern Lagos. Chaired and served on key finance-related committees.

2011–2015 · House of Representatives

Chairman, Public Accounts Committee

Appointed chairman of the House's only constitutionally-mandated committee as a first-term member, an early recognition of his accountancy expertise, leading the scrutiny of the Auditor-General's reports on federal spending.

2015–2023 · Senate, Lagos West

Chairman, Senate Committee on Finance

Two terms representing Nigeria's largest senatorial district by population. As Finance Chairman, he steered revenue frameworks, finance bills and the fiscal assumptions behind national budgets. Re-elected in 2019 with a renewed mandate.

2023–Present · Senate, Ogun West

Chairman, Senate Committee on Appropriations

Now chairs the committee through which every naira of the federal budget passes, the bridge between the executive's proposals and the legislature's power of the purse. Honoured as Commander of the Order of the Niger (CON).

Constituency Delivery: Ogun West & Beyond

In under three years as Senator for Ogun West, press reports document over 250 projects across the five LGAs, Ado-Odo/Ota, Imeko Afon, Ipokia, Yewa North and Yewa South, described by party leaders as unprecedented in the state's history.

Education

Classrooms built and renovated, school furniture supplied, libraries and ICT centres established across the district, paired with human investment: over 5,000 students supported with bursaries and scholarships.

Health

Primary healthcare facilities constructed and renovated across the five LGAs, bringing care closer to rural and border communities, alongside recurring free medical outreach programmes.

Water, Power & Light

Motorised and solar-powered boreholes bringing clean water to rural hubs; solar streetlights across rural and semi-urban communities; and electricity transformers supplied to over 100 communities to improve power supply.

Roads, Markets & Commerce

Strategic construction and rehabilitation of road networks to move farm produce to urban markets, plus modern market infrastructure, including the landmark Agbara and Igbesa ultramodern markets designed to boost cross-border commerce.

A comprehensive farm-support programme: tractors, ploughs, tricycles and water pumps; improved seeds, stems, seedlings, fertilisers and pesticides; plus direct cash grants reported at ₦100,000 each to 5,000 farmers.

Investment in the security of Ogun's border axis, including the construction of the Idiroko Police Station to strengthen border security in Ipokia, alongside streetlighting that makes communities safer after dark.
